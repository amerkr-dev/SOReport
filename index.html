Calculation Logic

DU gross sales = Current totaliser − Previous totaliser
DU net sales = DU gross sales − Testing quantity
Total DU sales = Sum of all DU net sales
Total available stock = Opening stock + Receipts
DIP sales = Total available stock − Closing physical/DIP stock
Variation = Total DU sales − DIP sales
Operational allowance = Closing tank stock × operational variation %
Evaporation/handling allowance = tiered calculation from annual-average sales and entered threshold/rates
Negative permissible limit = Operational allowance + Evaporation/handling allowance + applicable Shrinkage/TVA allowance
Negative margin = Negative permissible limit − absolute negative variation
The published MDG 2024 framework says stock reconciliation considers normal operational variation of 4% of tank stock, MS evaporation/handling loss, HSD handling loss, and shrinkage/temperature variation where applicable. The exact current rates and applicable TVA/shrinkage should be verified against the current official IOCL/OMC procedure before using this for an official inspection.
