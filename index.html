Data is stored locally in this browser. No server/database is required for GitHub Pages.
Calculation basis used by this app: Total DU sales = Σ(Current meter − Previous meter − Testing). Total available stock = Opening stock + Receipts. DIP sales = Total available stock − Closing physical/DIP stock. Variation = DU sales − DIP sales. Permissible variation is entered per product so the calculator does not assume a universal IOCL limit. Always use the applicable current IOCL guideline, approved tank calibration data and inspection methodology for official reconciliation.
